    Common questions

    Short answers, including the ones that matter.

    Getting started

    What is an airdrop?

    A distribution of tokens to addresses a project selects under its own rules. Eligibility can depend on past activity or holdings at a snapshot time. Each project sets its own rules and its own claim window.

    Which networks work here?

    Solana and Ethereum mainnet. Pick a network, paste an address, and the page reads that chain. Switching networks clears the address field.

    Why share it at all?

    One allocation is worth one address, not a fleet. A thousand addresses still need a thousand signatures, so the rest get used here instead of expiring.

    Is checking really read-only?

    Yes. A public RPC node answers with balances. Your wallet is not touched until you press the collect button.

    Results and amount

    How is the amount calculated?

    From what the address already holds and the slot it falls into. The fee sits right next to it.

    Is this free money?

    The airdrop is. The only cost is the network fee, printed above the button before anything opens.

    Where does the airdrop land?

    Allocations are released from the batch escrow and settle in the network's own coin, so they arrive as a transfer rather than a mint. Nothing is minted for you.

    Any limits?

    One claim per address per batch, first come first served. Obvious farming gets skipped.

    What if I click and change my mind?

    Cancel in the wallet and nothing is sent. A rejected signature costs zero.

    Do the numbers at the bottom describe me?

    No. They are historical figures from our other products. The amount in your own result comes only from your address, read live.

    Privacy and troubleshooting

    Why does Phantom say this site may be a scam?

    A brand-new hostname has no record yet, so wallets flag it as unproven. The simulation Phantom returns is the useful part: it matches the amount printed on this page exactly. Nothing is approved and left behind: one transfer per asset, plus one plain SOL transfer.

    MetaMask asked for my password and showed a recovery phrase — was that you?

    No. That is your wallet opening its own vault, and our domain simply sits in the tab behind it. We never see the phrase. If a site ever asks you to type it in, be suspicious; this one never does.

    Do I need a wallet to check?

    No. A pasted address is enough. A wallet is only needed for the collect step, because only it can sign.

    What happens to my address?

    It goes to a public RPC node to be read, and it is written into one cookieless hit record alongside the page path. No account, no cookie, no tracker id.

    What if the wallet rejects or nothing happens?

    A rejected signature costs nothing. If the wallet stays quiet, the button comes back after eight seconds so you can try again. A failed send is refunded by the network minus the fee.

    Will it work inside Telegram?

    The page works, but signing needs a real wallet. On mobile, open it inside the wallet’s own browser instead.

    Independent tool. Funds never pass through us — they go straight to the address shown. We never ask for a seed phrase.
