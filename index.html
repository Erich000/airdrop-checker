If something goes wrong

  • Where to look: the transaction stays in your wallet history. Open it there, or search the signature on a block explorer.

  • What it usually means: a rejected signature costs nothing. A failed send is refunded by the network, minus the fee.

  • Who to ask: send the signature and your address. Never send a seed phrase or a private key — to us or to anyone.
