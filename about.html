About

Who runs this

One person, one static page, no company behind it. That is the whole answer, and it is short on purpose.

Page live since 22 November 2026

What it is

A tool that reads a public wallet address and prints what it holds, on Solana and on Ethereum. Paste an address; no wallet, no signature, nothing moves while you look.

If you press claim, your wallet is asked to sign one transaction. It is a plain transfer, not a contract call: no mint, no approval, no delegation. What the wallet shows is what the four rows said.

What it is not

  • Not a project's official claim page. No token is issued here.
  • No account, no signup, no email.
  • No custody. Funds go straight to the address printed on the page, never to a middle step.
  • No paid tier and no upsell.

Contact

Things that are true but unflattering
